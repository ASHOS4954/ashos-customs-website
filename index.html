Built from a real export workflow.

AshOS Customs began as an internal tool created to reduce repetitive work involved in preparing export documentation.

It was designed around the practical challenges encountered when reviewing invoices, checking shipment details and producing commercial documentation for international orders.

The aim is straightforward: make export-document preparation more consistent, easier to review and less dependent on repeated manual entry.

Assistance, not automatic clearance.

AshOS assists with the preparation and review of export documentation. It does not provide legal or customs advice and cannot guarantee acceptance or customs clearance.

The exporter remains responsible for confirming the accuracy of product descriptions, values, origin, classification and all final shipment information.
